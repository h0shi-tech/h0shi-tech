## What I actually do

I build backend services and the tooling around them: parsers, internal APIs, integrations, and the small
CLIs that make those things observable and testable. Most of my day-to-day is Python and Go on Linux,
with PostgreSQL behind it.

The other half of my background is information security — PKI and TLS, key management, entropy and
side-channel coursework. That shows up in how I write code: explicit failure modes, least privilege,
no secrets in repos.
